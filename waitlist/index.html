How it works

  1. It asks one small question.

    “Where’s your head today?” Type a sentence, or hold the button and say it. That is all the effort there is.

  2. Your answer becomes a minute.

    It is written from your own words and read aloud over quiet music, in the voice you picked. The words show on screen as they are spoken.

  3. Every morning, it is waiting.

    Press play. On the days you skip the question, your minute still comes.

Pick the kind of minute you need

Before you join, the plain facts

It is a paid app.

$5.99 a week, about 86¢ a day. Or $59.99 a year, which is 16¢ a day. There is no free version, because every minute costs a writer and a voice. You see the price again before you pay anything.

AI makes it.

Each minute is written and voiced by AI, from your own words, inside rules about what it may say. What you write is stored encrypted and used for one thing: writing your minutes.

A prayer never speaks as God.

It is addressed to God as you understand God, in the tradition you choose. It does not claim to know God’s will.

It is not therapy.

It is a minute of encouragement. If what you write suggests you may be in danger, it shows you support resources instead.

Nothing nags you.

Miss a day or a month and you are met with a minute, not a lecture. There is no streak to buy back.
